    Lala the snail calls you to the garden: help us make ten

    In the garden there is a frame with ten nests. We will fill it with fireflies, make ten in different ways and solve short garden stories. You only need to press: with a mouse, with a finger or with the keys.

    How to play

    • Press the nests in the frame — a firefly sits in each one.
    • Everything you see has a word and a number: "seven", "7".
    • You can try as many times as you like. The "Hint" button is always near.
    • At the bottom of every card there is an "Again" button — it starts the activity from the beginning.

    Choose how you like to work

    «"More help": the nests are numbered, the first two steps glow, and the things in the stories have labels. You can switch at any time — it is not a mark.

    What we will learn

    • Numbers and amounts from zero to ten: "zero" is when the frame is empty, and that is an answer too.
    • The parts of ten: 0+10, 5+5 and other pairs.
    • Adding and taking away within ten with things you can see, and then with number sentences.
